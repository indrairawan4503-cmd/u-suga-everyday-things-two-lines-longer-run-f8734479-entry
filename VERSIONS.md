# Version history — variant A: TOASTER (one concept, iterated v1 → v8)

Concept: a toaster is a tanning booth for bread. One analogy only, one
concrete scene a 10-year-old can see and do (slide a slice in, watch it
brown, it pops up). Line 2 must name what the analogy maps AND what it
leaves out, specifically and honestly.

## v1 — 297 chars
A toaster is a tiny sunbed where a slice of bread lies down under glowing red bars to get a tan, then pops up brown and warm.
It matches because heat browns the bread like sunshine browns skin, but it leaves out that the bread cannot choose when to stop and the heat comes from wires, not the sun.
Note: first draft of the sunbed/tan analogy; plain, under budget, line 2 already names a limit (no choice, wires not sun).

## v2 — 352 chars
A toaster is a little sunbed where your bread slides down between glowing red bars for a quick tan, then pops up brown and warm when the timer dings.
It works because the hot bars brown the bread the way sunshine browns skin, but it leaves out that the bread cannot move or choose how brown it gets, and the heat comes from electric wires, not the sun.
Note: added the 10-year-old's own action (slides down), the timer, and "cannot move" to line 2; used more of the byte budget.

## v3 — 369 chars
A toaster is a little sunbed where your bread lies flat under glowing red bars for a quick tan, then pops up brown, warm, and crisp when its time in the sun is done.
It works because the hot bars brown the bread the way sunshine browns skin, but it leaves out that the bread cannot move, choose, or put on sunscreen, and the heat comes from electric wires, not the sun.
Note: added "crisp" (the texture payoff) and a playful sunscreen beat; near the top of the budget. But "lies flat" is wrong — bread stands upright in a toaster — and the sunscreen list is cute rather than honest.
